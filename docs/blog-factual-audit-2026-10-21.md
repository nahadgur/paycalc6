## Editorial basis

Salary examples assume regular cash pay, matching pensionable pay, resident personal relief only and February 2026 NSSF limits. Annual returns must use actual periods. This audit covers blog content and its routing/metadata, not a re-audit of all calculator code or standalone guide pages.
