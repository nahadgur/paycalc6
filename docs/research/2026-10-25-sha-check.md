## Deliberate exclusions and uncertainty

- No unverifiable 'My Contributions', 'Payment History' or 'Download Statement' button sequence. Afya Yangu's authenticated pages were not accessed. Article conditionally instructs checking any displayed transactions and requesting a record/payment trace if absent.
- No invented specific USSD menu numbers, guaranteed response time, SMS shortcodes, payment Paybill instructions or transfer account numbers.
- No guarantee of treatment eligibility based on payment status. No suggestion that an inactive result rules out all statutory care.
- No claim that registration confirmation proves remittance.
- No contribution rate or remittance deadline stated, because rates are not necessary to this article's task. The KES 1,375 example is explicitly hypothetical and is not presented as a universal amount.
- A Ministry of Health search result verifies a 147 hotline story (https://www.health.go.ke/cs-duale-visits-taifacare-support-centre-hub-147-hotline, turn14search0), but opening timed out. The article therefore uses the current SHA contact-page number, 0800 720 601, and does not rely on this inaccessible article.
