## Claims and primary sources

- https://www.labourmarket.go.ke/datalake/download/37/ — Ministry of Labour Employment Act copy, retrieved via the Download link at https://www.labourmarket.go.ke/data-catalogue/the-employment-act-2007/. Section 29, printed pages 39–40: three months full-pay maternity leave, written notice, medical certification on request, annual-leave preservation and return protection. Section 30, page 40: two-month qualification, seven full-pay then seven half-pay days, certification and notification, leave cycle and lawful deductions. The articles expressly require checking more favourable applicable wage orders/agreements; seven days is not presented as every employee's total entitlement. Additional maternity leave is not automatically represented as fully paid.
- https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/2007/11/eng%402022-12-31/source.pdf — Kenya Law consolidated Employment Act, cross-check of section 29 wording. Search of current discussions found proposals to extend maternity leave and a challenge to sick-leave qualification, not evidence of enacted replacement rules. Neither proposal is represented as enacted.
- https://www.kra.go.ke/individual/individual-pin-registration/93-tax-obligations — KRA explicitly includes salary, leave pay and sick pay in employment income and describes employer deduction/remittance responsibility. Used instead of intermittently timing-out general PAYE URL. No special maternity/sick-pay rate or tax exemption is invented.
- https://www.kra.go.ke/news-center/public-notices/2230-kra-simplifies-the-paye-return-filing-process — KRA notice dated 23 June 2025: simplified Excel return full rollout 1 July 2025 and official sample CSVs. Article asks employers to check the current template, not a hardcoded vendor schema.

Original practical examples, not statutory daily-rate prescriptions: 5 × 2000 × 50% = 5000 pay; 60000 − 5000 = 55000 revised gross. Maternity code example: 80000 salary relabelled remains 80000; duplication would produce 160000. Migration principal: 36000 − 6000 = 30000, while importing the post-payment amount and deducting again incorrectly gives 24000. No personal net-pay guarantee.
